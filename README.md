# 🔐 Digital Watermarking Service for Evidence Photos

A secure, web-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) solution that embeds verifiable, imperceptible watermarks into digital photographs to protect authenticity, ownership, and detect tampering. Built for institutions relying on digital photo evidence in legal discovery, law enforcement, insurance claims, and HR investigations.

---

## 🎯 Overview

Digital evidence photos are critical in legal and investigative contexts, yet they remain vulnerable to unauthorized modification using widely available image-editing tools. Traditional metadata-based protection is insufficient as metadata can be easily stripped or altered.

This service addresses these challenges by:

- Embedding **invisible, robust watermarks** that survive common image manipulations
- Providing **tamper detection** to identify if an image has been modified
- Offering **verification capabilities** to authenticate image ownership and integrity
- Supporting **multiple image formats** (JPEG, PNG, BMP, TIFF, DNG, RAW)
